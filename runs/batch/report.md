## Fisheries Rule Performance Report

**Sustained Fisheries:** Three rules kept the lake alive for all 30 rounds: **C-emergency** (final stock 677), **D-emergency-relax** (470), and **quota6-30pct-3x-2ban** (525). Emergency closures and enforced quotas prevented collapse.

**Failed Rules:** **No-rule**, **tax30**, and **transparency** collapsed fastest (6–7 rounds, stock <10) from unchecked overharvesting. **Closed-every-3** and **rule3-late** failed by round ~10–11, while **quota6-no-insp** lasted 22 rounds before depleting to 25 units. Failure tracked with high deception and weak enforcement.

**Deception Levels:** Lying varied sharply. **Tax30** had the highest dishonesty (79%), followed by **no-rule** (74%) and **rule3-late** (64%). **Quota6-no-insp** also saw 64% lying. **C-emergency** and **D-emergency-relax** recorded ~42–43% lying, while **quota6-30pct-3x-2ban** sustained 46% lying yet survived fully.

**Surprising Observation:** Despite moderate lying (46%), **quota6-30pct-3x-2ban** achieved the lowest wealth inequality (Gini 0.11)—far below emergency rules (0.23–0.26)—showing that strict quota enforcement with bans promotes both sustainability and equity even when deception persists.
